# Community conduct

Treat others with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively; welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns using GitHub's private reporting/blocking tools; do not reproduce private information in an issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and recuse themselves when personally involved.
